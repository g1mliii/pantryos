Motion, once and for all

One rule keeps every menu, dialog and toast fast: animate only opacity and transform. Both are composited on the GPU and never trigger layout. Animating height, top, width or box-shadow forces a reflow on every frame and is what makes menus feel cheap.

--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)
--ease-in: cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)
--enter: 140ms var(--ease-out)
--exit: 90ms var(--ease-in)

Non-negotiable

Exit is always faster than enter — 90ms out, 140ms in. Slow dismissals feel broken.

Transform origin follows the anchor, so a menu grows out of its trigger rather than floating in.

Keep the panel mounted and toggle visibility, or the exit transition never plays.

Honour prefers-reduced-motion — drop to 1ms, keep the opacity fade.

Never animate on first paint. Only in response to a real interaction.
